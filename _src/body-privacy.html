Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

What we collect

If you contact us through this site, we receive whatever you choose to send us: your name, company, email address, phone number and the details of your enquiry. The forms on this site send what you enter to our team by email through our own form handler. Nothing is added to a marketing list.

How we use it

Solely to reply to your enquiry, prepare an audit or quote, and — if you become a client — to deliver the work. We do not sell, rent or share your details with third parties for marketing.

Cookies and advertising

This site shows ads through Google AdSense. Google and its partners are third-party vendors that use cookies and similar identifiers to serve ads based on your visits to this site and other sites on the internet. When you first visit, a banner asks whether you accept these cookies. Until you accept, advertising and analytics cookies stay switched off and only what the site needs to work is kept, including the record of your choice.

You can change your choice at any time in cookie settings. You can also turn off personalised advertising from Google in Google Ads Settings, or opt out of personalised advertising from other participating vendors at aboutads.info. For how Google uses information from sites that use its services, see Google's partner-sites policy.

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser makes a request to Google's servers; Google's own privacy policy applies to that request.

Client data

Where a project gives us access to your accounts, systems or customer data, we treat it as confidential, restrict access to the people working on your project, and return or remove our access when the engagement ends.

Your rights

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Write to info@trendwalkers.dpdns.org and we will action it within 30 days.
