LinkedIn for Founders: What to Post When You Have No Time

Published 13 November 2026 · Trend Walkers team

Founders are told to "build a personal brand" and given no usable instruction. Here is a system that takes about an hour a week and is built to produce conversations, not applause.

Start with the profile, not the posts

Posting sends people to your profile. If the profile does not say what you do and for whom, the visit is wasted. Fix three things first: a headline that states the outcome you deliver and for which customer, an About section written for a buyer rather than a recruiter, and a featured link to one useful thing, such as a case study or a booking page.

Four kinds of post that work

  • What we learned. A specific lesson from real work, including what went wrong. These build trust faster than anything else.
  • How we do it. One part of your process, explained plainly. Buyers want to know what working with you is like.
  • What I disagree with. A common belief in your industry that you think is wrong, and why. This attracts the customers who think like you.
  • What a customer asked. A real question and your honest answer. If one buyer asked, others are wondering.

How often

Two posts a week, every week, beats five posts one week and silence the next. Consistency matters more than frequency. Write four posts in one sitting once a fortnight and schedule them.

What to avoid

  • Engagement pods. The comments are from people who will never buy.
  • Posts written to go viral. Reach among strangers is not pipeline.
  • Generic motivational content. It gets likes and no enquiries.
  • Outsourcing the voice entirely. An editor can tidy your writing. Nobody else can have your opinions.

Turning views into conversations

Most leads from LinkedIn do not come from the post. They come from what you do afterwards. Reply to every real comment. Look at who viewed your profile each week and connect with the ones who match your customer. Send a short, specific message with no pitch. The post earns the right to start the conversation; it does not replace it.

What to measure

Ignore impressions. Track three numbers: profile views from your target customer type, connection requests received, and conversations started. If those rise for eight weeks, the system is working.
