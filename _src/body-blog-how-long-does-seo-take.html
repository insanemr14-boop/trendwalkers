How Long Does SEO Take to Work? An Honest Timeline

Published 13 November 2026 · Trend Walkers team

Most agencies answer this with "three to six months" and move on. The honest answer depends on what is broken, what you are competing against and what you count as working.

What actually has to happen

Search engines have to crawl your changes, reprocess the pages, and then test them against the pages already ranking. Each step has its own delay, and none of them can be bought or rushed.

That is why SEO is slow in a way paid advertising is not. An ad starts showing the hour you pay for it. A page earns its position, and loses it, gradually.

A realistic timeline

Weeks 1 to 4: fix what is broken. Technical audit, indexing problems, slow pages, missing titles, broken internal links. Nothing visible happens to traffic yet. This is the phase clients find hardest, because the invoice arrives before the graph moves.

Months 2 to 3: early signal. Pages that were not indexed start appearing. Impressions rise before clicks do. Long, specific searches begin to rank. If nothing at all has moved by the end of month three, something is wrong with the plan.

Months 4 to 6: compounding. Pages that entered the top twenty start moving into the top ten. Traffic growth becomes visible in analytics without squinting. Enquiries from search begin to show up.

Month 6 onwards: the return. Work done in month two is still paying. This is the part that makes SEO cheaper than ads over a long enough period.

What speeds it up, and what slows it down

  • Faster: an established domain, a site that was technically broken (fixes show quickly), low-competition local searches, and content that already exists but is badly organised.
  • Slower: a brand new domain, a crowded national market, a site rebuilt on a new platform, or a history of bought links that has to be cleaned up.

How to tell early if it is working

Do not wait six months to find out. These signals show up within the first eight to ten weeks:

  • The number of indexed pages is going up.
  • Impressions in Google Search Console are rising, even if clicks are flat.
  • You are appearing for more distinct searches than before.
  • Average position on your target pages is improving, even from 60 to 30.

If your agency cannot show you these numbers, ask why. They are free and available to anyone with access to the property.

When SEO is the wrong choice

If you need leads this month, SEO will not deliver them. Run paid search or paid social for immediate demand, and build organic search alongside it so that you are not renting every visitor forever. We tell clients this before they sign, not after.
