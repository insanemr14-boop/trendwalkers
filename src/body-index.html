FAQ

The questions we get asked first.

How soon will I see results?

Paid campaigns and reels can move within weeks. SEO is a 4–6 month build in most competitive niches — anyone promising page one in 30 days is either buying links or lying. We tell you which lever is fast and which is slow before you sign.

Do you work on retainer or per project?

Both. Websites, apps and brand identity are fixed-scope projects. SEO, social and ads work as monthly retainers because they compound. Many clients start with a project and add a retainer after launch.

Can I hire you for just one service?

Yes. Plenty of clients come to us only for reel editing or only for LinkedIn. We'll tell you honestly if that single channel is unlikely to move your number on its own.

Who owns the work?

You do — code, design files, content, ad accounts and analytics, all in your name from day one.

Do you work with businesses outside India?

Yes. We work remotely across time zones, with calls scheduled in your working hours and everything documented in writing.

What does it cost?

It depends on scope, and we won't pretend otherwise. The audit is free and ends with a written, itemised quote — no obligation, no pressure call.
