About us

We started Trend Walkers because most brands were being sold the wrong thing.

Reports full of impressions. Websites that looked good and ranked nowhere. Reels edited beautifully for an account nobody followed. Activity billed as progress.

What we believe

Growth is a system, not a channel.

A brand doesn't grow because its Instagram is pretty or because it ranks for one keyword. It grows when someone can find you, understand you and buy from you without friction — and every one of those steps sits with a different vendor at most companies.

Trend Walkers exists to hold all of it in one place. We do the search work, the social work, the creative and the engineering, so the strategy survives contact with implementation.

We say no

If a channel won't work for your business, we say so before invoicing for it. Losing a line item beats losing a client six months in when nothing moved.

We show the work

You get access to the queue, the drafts, the analytics and the repo. If you want to know what we did on a Tuesday, you can just look.

We don't do shortcuts

No bought links, no engagement pods, no follower packages. Everything we do is meant to still be working after the next algorithm update.

We report the misses

Every monthly review includes what underperformed and why. An agency that only reports wins is editing, not reporting.

We keep teams small

Fewer accounts, senior people on each one. You won't be handed to a junior after the pitch call.

We plan in 90 days

Long enough for compounding channels like SEO to show signal, short enough that you're never locked into a strategy that stopped working.
